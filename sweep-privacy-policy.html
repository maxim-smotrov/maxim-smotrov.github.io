Sweep Privacy Policy

Last update date: September 11, 2026

Sweep is a macOS app that maps disk space and helps you find and remove files. This policy applies to both the direct download and App Store versions.

No tracking

Sweep does not track you, show ads, or include analytics or third-party crash reporting tools. There is no account or login. The app does not send your files, scan results, or local logs to us or to third-party servers.

Local scans and file access

Sweep reads file and folder information, including names, paths, types, and disk usage, to build a disk map on your Mac. Scanning uses file metadata rather than reading document contents. If you choose to preview a file, Sweep opens it with macOS Quick Look. You can also reveal files in Finder, move them to the Trash, or delete them permanently.

The App Store version asks you to select a folder and saves permission to access it again. The direct download version uses the file access available to the app. Sweep may ask you to enable Full Disk Access to scan protected locations. You can manage Full Disk Access in System Settings under Privacy & Security.

Data stored on your Mac

Sweep stores some information locally to support its features:

  • Scan history records the path, completion time, and duration of the last scan for each scanned location. The full disk map is kept in memory and is not saved between launches.
  • The App Store version saves folder access bookmarks in local preferences so you do not need to select the same folder each time. The direct download version does not save these bookmarks.
  • Window size and position are saved so Sweep can restore them when you reopen the app.
  • Error logs contain timestamps and error details, which may include file or folder paths. Sweep keeps a current log and one previous log in the Sweep folder under your home folder's Library/Logs directory. It also writes diagnostic messages to the macOS system log.

Scan history and saved folder permissions remain in local preferences until removed. Older error logs are replaced as the log grows. Sweep does not upload or synchronize this information. Removing the app alone may leave local preferences and logs on your Mac.
